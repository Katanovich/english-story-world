# Course Plan: English Story World, A1 → C2

The full curriculum: **6 levels × 2 units × 5 lessons = 60 lessons.** Each lesson has
about 13–18 exercises across all sections (words, reading, listening, grammar, speaking,
quiz). Every lesson reviews the previous lesson (RECENT_REVIEW) and selected older
material (OLDER_REVIEW). The last lesson of each unit has a cumulative quiz.

Characters are placeholders, filled in from `backend/app/seed/characters.py`
(public defaults) or the private `characters.local.json`:

| Placeholder | Role | Public default |
|---|---|---|
| `{boy}` | main hero, about 9 at A1; grows older level by level | Alex |
| `{little}` | his little sister, 5 at A1 | Mia |
| `{teen}` | his older sister, a teenager | Kate |
| `{dad}` | father | Tom |
| `{mum}` | mother | Anna |
| `{gran1}`, `{gran2}` | the two grandmothers | Rose, Lily |
| `{aunt}` | aunt (mum's sister) | Emma |

**Story arc:** the family grows up with the learner. `{little}` learns to read (A2), starts
school (B1), and shares her own stories (C1). `{teen}` goes on an exchange year (B2) and
starts university (C1). `{boy}` moves from family life to clubs, science, the wider
world, and finally his own projects. Content stays child-appropriate at every level.

## A1 · Beginner ✅

**Unit 1: Little Explorers.** Meet My Family · My Home · My Little Pet · Colors and Toys ·
Where Is My Ball?

**Unit 2: Our Big Family.** My Big Family (he/she, big/little/old/young) · Happy Birthday!
(numbers 1–10, How old…?) · Lunch at Granny's (food, I don't like, Do you like…?) ·
Sunny or Rainy? (clothes, weather, Put on…) · I Can Swim! (actions, can/can't; A1 review)

## A2 · Elementary: "Everyday Adventures" ✅

**Unit 1: School Days**
1. My School: school objects and rooms; *there is / there are*
2. My Day: daily routine, telling the time; present simple with he/she (-s)
3. Sports Day: sports; adverbs of frequency (always, sometimes, never)
4. Right Now!: `{little}` at kindergarten; present continuous
5. At the Shop: shopping, money, *How much…?*; some/any; unit review

**Unit 2: The Family Trip**
6. Packing Our Bags: holiday things; *going to* for plans
7. On the Train: transport, directions; prepositions of movement
8. At Granny's Village: farm animals; past simple of *be* (was/were)
9. What Did You Do?: past simple, regular and common irregular verbs
10. A Postcard Home: comparatives; writing a postcard; A2 review

## B1 · Intermediate: "Growing Up" ✅

**Unit 1: Clubs and Friends**
1. The Science Club: experiments; present perfect (ever/never, just/already)
2. `{teen}`'s Band: music and preferences; *would like*, *prefer*
3. Cooking with Aunt `{aunt}`: recipes, quantities; first conditional
4. The Lost Kitten: past continuous vs past simple; telling a story
5. The School Play: feelings; *must / have to / don't have to*; unit review

**Unit 2: Our Town**
6. Save the Park!: environment; *should*; giving advice
7. When Granny Was Young: an interview; *used to*
8. A Day at the Museum: history; present and past passive
9. Planning a Party: invitations; *will* vs *going to*
10. The Town Newspaper: news; reported speech basics; B1 review

## B2 · Upper-intermediate: "Big Ideas" ✅

**Unit 1: Explorers of Science**
1. Space Week: the solar system; second conditional
2. Robots and Us: technology; defining and non-defining relative clauses
3. The Weather Station: climate; present perfect continuous
4. Healthy Choices: health and sport; gerunds vs infinitives
5. The Invention Fair: presenting an idea; the passive in all tenses; unit review

**Unit 2: Around the World**
6. Pen Friends: cultures; contrast linkers (although, however, whereas)
7. `{teen}`'s Exchange Year: third conditional; *wish / if only*
8. Travel Troubles: polite complaints and requests; indirect questions
9. Festivals of the World: traditions; articles in depth
10. The Debate Club: opinions and arguments; B2 review

## C1 · Advanced: "Making a Difference" ✅

**Unit 1: Media and Projects**
1. The Documentary: narrative tenses for effect
2. Fact or Fake?: media literacy; hedging and cautious language
3. The Eco Project: cleft sentences and emphasis
4. `{little}`'s First Story: perspective and voice; mixed conditionals
5. The Charity Run: persuasion; inversion after negative adverbials; unit review

**Unit 2: Voices and Futures**
6. Granny's Memories: life writing; past perfect continuous
7. Code and Creativity: abstract nouns and collocations
8. Job Shadow Day: formal register in workplace English
9. Thinking Machines: discussion; past modals of deduction
10. Presentation Day: discourse markers and structure; C1 review

## C2 · Proficiency: "Mastery"

**Unit 1: The Art of Language**
1. Idioms at the Dinner Table: idiomatic and figurative language
2. Reading the Classics: literary analysis vocabulary
3. Humour and Wordplay: puns, irony, understatement
4. The Academic Essay: nominalisation and an academic style
5. The Formal Debate: rhetoric and rebuttal; unit review

**Unit 2: Our Story**
6. Translating Granny's Tales: register and cultural nuance
7. Science for Everyone: popularising complex ideas
8. The Negotiation: diplomacy and tact
9. Poetry and Rhythm: sound, stress and style
10. The Family Film: the whole family's story, retold; final course review
